Golden POS V6 — Merged Table Bills + Modern Owner Dashboard

PAYMENT / TABLE BILLING
- Table payment groups now have only two merge actions: Merge all, or Choose tickets to merge.
- Choosing tickets creates a persistent merged bill in the database; the original kitchen orders remain separate.
- A merged bill appears as one selectable ticket in the normal Payment list with its calculated final amount.
- Selecting the merged bill uses the regular payment detail panel, cash/card/online method, tender keypad, and confirmation workflow.
- Merged bills support percent or fixed discounts, reasons, manager approval PIN rules, editing, and removal before payment begins.
- Partial payments are allocated across the member tickets and fully-paid member tickets remain traceable as individual kitchen orders.
- A merged bill can be printed from the Payment panel. This is a combined bill printout, not a separate fiscal invoice number.
- Table bills are persisted in SQLite. The schema migration is additive and does not replace existing restaurant data.

DASHBOARD

- Preserves existing dashboard/report API data and period filters.
