GOLDEN POS - START HERE
=======================

TO OPEN THE POS
  Double-click  "Golden POS"  on the desktop  (or  OPEN-POS.cmd  in this folder).
  If the POS is not running it starts it for you - just wait for the
  "Opening Golden POS..." window. The very first time it also sets itself
  up (needs internet, about 10 minutes, only once).

INSTALL ON THE POS TERMINAL (once)
  1. Install Node.js 22 LTS from nodejs.org and Google Chrome.
  2. Install both receipt printers in Windows. Plug the cash drawer
     cable into the BILL printer.
  3. Double-click  INSTALL-POS.cmd  and click "Yes" when Windows asks.
     Wait until it says DONE.
  4. The POS opens full-screen. Log in as Admin, go to
     Settings > Printer management: tick "Enable direct thermal printing",
     choose the kitchen and payment printer, Save, then Test both.
  5. Restart the computer once to check it all comes back by itself.

EVERY DAY
  Nothing to do. Turn the computer on - the POS starts by itself.
  Closed it by mistake? Double-click "Golden POS" on the desktop.
  Bills print and the cash drawer opens automatically.

TABLE QR ORDERING (once)
  1. In the Wi-Fi router, reserve this computer's IP address (see
     HOSTING-LOCAL-WINDOWS.md > Table QR ordering) so it never changes.
  2. Settings > Online ordering & QR: press "Use this computer's address",
     enter the Wi-Fi name + password, Save.
  3. Floor > Print all table QR cards. Put one on each table.
  Orders from tables pop up on the cashier screen: Online Orders >
  Table Orders > Accept & send to kitchen.
  Customers on MOBILE DATA too: double-click SETUP-ONLINE-ORDERING.cmd,
  then paste the https address it gives into the QR code address.

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
  Double-click "Golden POS Control" on the desktop:
    1 = check it's running      2 = restart it
    5 = backup now              8 = leave full-screen

WHERE THINGS ARE (C:\GoldenPOS)
  backend\data\pos.db       all your sales, menu and staff
  backend\data\backups\     a copy of the database every day (30 days)
  backend\data\print-log\   every bill and kitchen ticket, by date

UPDATE TO A NEW VERSION
  Unzip the new version anywhere and double-click its INSTALL-POS.cmd.
  Your data is kept.

More detail: HOSTING-LOCAL-WINDOWS.md
