Golden POS V4 — Dashboard & Reports enhancements

Implemented in the actual Golden POS Next.js UI and backend API:
- Dashboard date-range business analytics panel with Load action.
- Period KPIs: net sales, paid orders, average order, discounts, inventory purchases, recorded stock consumption.
- Sales breakdown by order type, menu category, and employee.
- Reports page now includes sales by order type and sales by employee.
- CSV export includes the new order-type and employee breakdowns.
- Existing reports remain: payment method, hourly sales, top items, categories, weekday performance, waste, voids, modifiers, dead stock, inventory purchase/consumption, PDF/print export.
- Classic HTML dashboard and reports also include the period analytics and order-type/employee report sections.

Data accuracy notes:
- Revenue and order-type/employee analytics are calculated from paid orders in the selected date range.
- Inventory purchases and consumption reflect records already present in the inventory system.
- Food cost %, gross profit, net profit, labor cost, forecasts, customer retention, and tax analytics are not fabricated. They require reliable recipe costs, operating-expense/payroll records, customer history, and/or tax configuration before they can be reported accurately.

Install/run using the existing project instructions. Preserve backend/data/pos.db when replacing application files; back it up first.
